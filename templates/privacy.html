Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use the Service, we store the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is needed to operate the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Spendly experience. Specifically, we use it to display your expense history, generate spending summaries, and maintain your account.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond delivering the Service to you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Upon deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the Service, but these providers are contractually bound to keep your data confidential.

We do not integrate with third-party advertising networks or analytics platforms that track you across other websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or wish to exercise your rights regarding your personal data, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website.
